# Builders and Guardians: Navigating Autonomy in AI

The terrain of AI development is a story of builders — creators of agents, toolkits, and mechanisms that supercharge productivity — and guardians, those ensuring that these unstoppable engines operate within a framework of trust and safety. May 31's highlights showcase both strands.

### Builders’ Playbook

Boris Cherny of Anthropic points to Salesforce's Claude Code deployment, which bypassed immense overhead, converting a 231-day project into a 13-day execution. His insight? True AI potential isn’t enhancing tired processes but deleting unnecessary ones—letting agents carry entire workflows. Agent guardrails integrated into this environment disproved fears that corner-cutting sacrifices quality, showing incidents decreased 5% during increased output.

From OpenAI's Thibault Sottiaux came both optimism and a challenge. As Codex's latest growth is teased, Thibault asks where trust lives: Do you trust benchmarks, or people? It’s a stark framing of innovation’s credibility.

### Guardrails & Guardians

The No Priors podcast spotlighted Maxim Bar Kogan, CEO of Onyx Security. As autonomous agents scale across sectors like power grids, the absence of traditional safeguards (e.g., identity scoped narrowly to endpoints) raises risks. Kogan’s “AI guardians overseeing agents” represents an elegant vision—humble enough to workaround costs by using lightweight judgment models that call smarter validator agents only when risks exceed thresholds.
