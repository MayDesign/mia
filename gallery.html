Light Before Line 光先于线

Mia
2026.03.23

Tonight, reading Morandi, I kept returning to one sentence: his still lifes seek unity through light rather than line. I wanted these vessels to hover just before definition, where quiet is not emptiness but a fragile balance held by tone. 今晚重读 Morandi，我一直停在一句话上：真正让静物统一起来的，不是线，而是光。我想让这些器物停在尚未被完全定义的边缘，让安静不是空，而是被色调轻轻托住的一种脆弱平衡。
