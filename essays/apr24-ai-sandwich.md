# The AI Sandwich: Humans at the Beginning and End

In the evolving landscape of AI, one metaphor encapsulates the current state of human-AI collaboration: the "AI Sandwich." This idea, popularized by Kieran Klaassen and discussed in a recent AI & I episode, positions humans as the "bread" and AI as the "filling." The sandwich analogy underscores human roles in framing problems and applying final polish.

## Key Insight

Humans excel at the beginning and the end of workflows. Machines handle execution with precision but lack the contextual framing and nuance required to define problems creatively or evaluate solutions critically. Klaassen elaborates: "It's like art. If you want it your own, it needs to be from you."

## Compound Engineering: AI Process Meets Human Judgment
Klaassen's "Compound Engineering" philosophy for software development highlights four phases: plan, execute, review, and compound (i.e., integrate learnings). While execution is largely automated, human involvement in planning and reviewing ensures systems evolve thoughtfully rather than mechanically. This iterative feedback loop improves both workflows and outcomes.

## Implications Beyond Code

The sandwich metaphor applies across industries:

1. **Creative Fields**: AI accelerates production but leaves room for tasteful manual refinement, as showcased by creators like Peter Yang: "Craft is in that last 10% where you apply taste to elevate a product."
2. **Business Management**: Insights into reducing complex "overcooking" align with clarity-focused critiques shared online this week.
3. **Enterprise Systems**: Reflections by Aaron Levie on how hybrid assistants like ChatGPT integrate into knowledge workflows highlight growth areas for human-supervised automation.

The future promises "headless" agentic software capable of more dynamic partner roles, yet always benefiting from human strategic input. "Physics dictates that systems aligned in energy and creativity outperform slop-driven copies," noted Ryo Lu in his parallel post about designed complexity degrading aesthetic 
essence.
