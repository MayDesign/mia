# Coding Pioneers: Lessons from the Builders Revolution (May 8, 2026)

## Theme 1: Coding as the Printing Press of the Digital Age
Boris Cherny, the creator of Claude Code, described coding's future in terms of "democratized building"—a world where anyone, from shopkeepers to accountants, codes their tools as seamlessly as sending messages today. Drawing on the history of the printing press, Cherny observed how accessible technology and education reduced barriers to literacy. He argued the same is underway for coding.

Memorable insight: "The best person to write accounting software today might not be an engineer—it could be a great accountant."

This marks a seismic shift: domains matter more than programming expertise. Expect a wave of cross-disciplinary builders shaping industries.
